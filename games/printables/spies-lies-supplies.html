Supreme Commander (P1)
TURN SEQUENCE:
On your turn, perform exactly ONE action:
  1. Deploy: Play 1 card from hand face-up into its matching theater.
  2. Improvise: Play 1 card from hand face-down into ANY theater (Strength = 2).

Spies, Lies & Supplies Rules
FACE-DOWN CARDS (LIES):
  • Base Strength is always 2 (unless modified).
  • Have no abilities and no theater type.
  • Can be played into any theater.
  • If flipped face-up later, their Tactical ability does NOT trigger.
ABILITY KEYWORDS:
⚡ Tactical: Triggers immediately when played face-up from hand.
∞ Ongoing: Continuous effect active as long as the card is face-up.
TIE-BREAKER: If total strength in a theater is tied, the first player who played a card there wins the tie!
